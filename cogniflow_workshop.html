Missing standards

Data formats, metadata, and processing steps are often project-specific, making exchange and reuse difficult.

Difficult digitalization

Laboratory workflows are heterogeneous and hard to connect across instruments, software tools, and automation systems.

Limited reproducibility

Processing decisions, parameter choices, and intermediate results are not always captured in a traceable way.

Need for extensibility

Frameworks must remain flexible enough to integrate new functionalities, algorithms, and domain-specific processing tools.
