Running Regression

Sample Result Stability Across Neighboring Calibration Sessions

Pre-treatment and post-treatment samples are each evaluated once with the local session calibration and once with the mean result from 11 neighboring calibration windows to assess robustness across nearby measurement sessions.
about Standard Errors Both the pre-treatment and post-treatment results remain comparable when the calibration is averaged across +/- 5 measurement sessions. The standard errors of the 11-session means likely provide a more realistic & robust estimate of the uncertainty for a practical sample measurement.

qPeaks: A Linear Regression-Based Asymmetric Peak Model for Parameter-Free Automatized Detection and Characterization of Chromatographic Peaks in Non-Target Screening Data

Max Reuschenbach, Felix Drees, Michael S. Leupold, Lucie K. Tintrop, Torsten C. Schmidt, Gerrit Renner

Analytical Chemistry, 96(18), 7120-7129, 2024

This article introduces qPeaks, a linear regression-based asymmetric peak model for automated chromatographic peak detection and characterization in non-target screening. It is directly relevant here because it connects transformed peak models with efficient parameter estimation and uncertainty-aware peak analysis.

qPeaks for microplastics via TED-GC-MS

Automated, parameter-free peak detection and peak fitting for fast and traceable TED-GC-MS data evaluation.
Extremely fast

> 90 million regressions s−1
one measurement evaluated in less than one second

Detection + fitting

Automatic peak detection and asymmetric peak fitting in a single workflow.

Parameter-free

No manual input parameters are required for routine data evaluation.

Peak properties

Peak area, peak position, retention time and peak height are extracted automatically.

Uncertainty-aware

Uncertainties of computed quantities are estimated together with the peak descriptors.

Take-home message

TED-GC-MS is not a push-button method but with targeted method development and robust data evaluation, it has strong potential for microplastics analysis.
